# ICAT Assessment Tool (v1.1)

A free, single-file web app for recognizing and documenting suspected concussion in industrial and occupational settings. Built for safety managers, first aiders, injury prevention specialists, and onsite medical professionals at any worksite.

**Use it online:** [industrialconcussion.org/icat](https://industrialconcussion.org/icat)

## What it does
- Two role-based paths: First Responder (recognition, red flags, escalation, documentation) and Clinical Assessment (vitals and GCS, transition to onsite medical, symptom inventory, orientation and cognition, neuro and cervical screen, tandem gait and mVOMS).
- Guides the user from incident through an EMS, Remove From Duty, or Monitor outcome.
- Generates a signature-ready PDF record.
- No accounts and no data storage. Everything stays in the browser session.

To run it offline, download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser.

## Privacy
- The app stores no assessment data. Worker and assessment details are never sent anywhere; they stay in your browser session, and the PDF is generated on your device.
- The report screen includes an optional "Stay connected with ICI" form. If you choose to submit it, only the contact details you enter (name, email, and optional organization and role, plus a timestamp and the app version) are sent to the Industrial Concussion Institute via [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co). No assessment data is included.
